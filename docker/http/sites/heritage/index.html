A Century in the Making

1952

The Founding

Heinrich Voss, a trained watchmaker from Biel, establishes Chronova S.A. in Le Sentier, Vallée de Joux. The first workshop employs three artisans and produces 24 watches in its inaugural year.

1961

First In-House Calibre

After nine years of development, Chronova unveils the CV-100 — its first entirely manufacture movement. The automatic calibre beats at 18,000 vph and offers a 42-hour power reserve.

1974

The Nautique

Responding to growing demand from professional divers, Chronova launches the Nautique with 300m water resistance. It becomes the brand's first commercial success beyond Switzerland.

1989

Second Generation

Klaus Voss inherits the manufacture from his father. Under his leadership, Chronova doubles its workforce and opens its first boutique on the Rue du Rhône, Geneva.

2008

Calibre CV-3200

Chronova introduces the CV-3200 — a silicon-escapement automatic with 72 hours of power reserve and 847 individual components. Entirely designed and manufactured in-house.

2024

Today

Under the third generation of the Voss family, Chronova produces approximately 4,000 timepieces annually. Every watch leaves the manufacture only after a 72-hour continuous regulation test.
