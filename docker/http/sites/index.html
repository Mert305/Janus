Seven Decades of Swiss Mastery

Founded in 1952 by Heinrich Voss in the Vallée de Joux, Chronova has remained family-owned through three generations. Each movement is assembled by a single master watchmaker — a tradition we call montre à la main.

Our calibre CV-3200 — beating at 4 Hz with a 72-hour power reserve — is entirely manufactured in-house, from mainspring to escapement wheel.
